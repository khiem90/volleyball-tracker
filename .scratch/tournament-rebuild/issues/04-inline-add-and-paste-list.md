# 04: Inline add and paste list

**What to build:** On the Teams page, typing a name and pressing Enter saves the team and puts the
cursor back in the field. A paste-a-list action adds one team per line. Quick
Add with generated names is removed.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Ten names typed with Enter between them produce ten teams without opening a dialog.
- [ ] Pasting five lines adds five teams; blank lines are ignored and names that already exist are reported rather than duplicated.
- [ ] Each new team gets a color, and the new row is visible after adding.
- [ ] Quick Add and its generated names are gone.
