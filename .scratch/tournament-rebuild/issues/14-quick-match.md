# 14: Quick match

**What to build:** A signed-in owner picks two roster teams for a quick match, sees abandoned quick
matches with Resume and Discard, and quick match results count toward team
records. A guest gets a working Play Again.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Quick page offers a roster pick for both teams and starts scoring.
- [ ] A quick match left mid-way shows on the Quick page with Resume and Discard; Resume reopens it, Discard removes it.
- [ ] A completed quick match appears in the team's all-time record.
- [ ] After a guest match, Play Again starts a fresh guest match; closing the result dialog does not strand the page.
